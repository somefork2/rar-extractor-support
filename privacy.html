Privacy Policy

RAR Extractor for macOS. Last updated 20 September 2026.

The short version

The app collects nothing, sends nothing and has no way to. It has no account, no analytics, and no network access at all — the App Sandbox it ships in does not grant it the entitlement that would allow a network connection, so it could not reach us even if it tried.

What stays on your Mac

The app remembers a few things, all of them locally and all of them listed in Settings ▸ Privacy, where each can be cleared:

  • the archives you opened recently, by name and path;
  • a running total of how much space compression has saved you;
  • the folders you have allowed it to write into, as macOS security-scoped bookmarks;
  • archive passwords, but only those you asked it to remember, and only in your login keychain.

The contents of your archives are read to show and extract them, and are never copied anywhere but where you ask.

What we receive

Nothing. We have no server. If you write to support, we see your message and whatever you choose to put in it, and we use it only to answer you.

Apple

The app is sold through the Mac App Store, so Apple handles the purchase and may share aggregate sales and crash statistics with us. That is Apple's collection, under Apple's privacy policy, not ours, and it never identifies you to us.

Children

The app is a file utility. It does not gather personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
